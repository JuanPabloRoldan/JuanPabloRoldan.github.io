### About me
I am a Ph.D. student in aerospace engineering at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU), working in the Computational Fluids and Aerodynamics Laboratory (CFAL).
My primary research is on shock-induced droplet deformation and aerobreakup, with a focus on how entrapped non-condensable gas and seeded cavitation nuclei change which surface instability wavelengths get selected.
I hold a dual M.S. from ERAU and Universidad del Valle (UV), where I remain part of the IMPETUS INDOMITUS team.
I have performed visiting research at NASA Ames working on hypersonic aerothermodynamics for the Dragonfly aeroshell and at DLR Cologne on reduced-order modeling for compressor cascade optimization.
