## Presentations

#### Numerical Investigation of Shock-Induced Deformation of Bubble-Laden Droplets

[Slides (PDF)](/papers/msthesis26_defense.pdf) [PPTX](/papers/msthesis26_defense.pptx) [Thesis](https://commons.erau.edu/edt/1019)

*M.S. Thesis Defense, Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University &mdash; Summer 2026*

Defense of my M.S. thesis, carried out jointly with the Computational Fluids and Aerodynamics Lab at ERAU (Dr. Michael Kinzel) and IMPETUS INDOMITUS at Universidad del Valle (Dr. Guillermo Jaramillo). Across 36 gas-seeded cases, bubble-induced pressure perturbations produced no measurable change in early-time droplet deformation, which stays governed by external aerodynamic loading.

#### CFD-Informed Linear-to-Nonlinear Instability Growth in High-Mach-Number Drop Aerobreakup

[Slides (PDF)](/papers/hydroinstab26_final.pdf) [PPTX](/papers/hydroinstab26_final.pptx)

*Final project presentation, Hydrodynamic Instabilities (780125), Universidad del Valle &mdash; July 2026*
